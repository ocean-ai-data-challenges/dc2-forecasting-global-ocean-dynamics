# Further reading

Every reference below was checked against its DOI.

## Data Challenge & PPR Océan & Climat

- [PPR Océan & Climat project website](https://www.ocean-climat.fr/)
- [DC2 GitHub repository](https://github.com/ocean-ai-data-challenges/dc2-forecasting-global-ocean-dynamics)
- [dc-tools evaluation library](https://github.com/ocean-ai-data-challenges/dc-tools)
- [OceanBench](https://github.com/mercator-ocean/oceanbench), Mercator Ocean's evaluation
  library, whose Class 4 evaluator DC2 uses

## Baselines

- El Aouni, A., Gaudel, Q., Regnier, C., Van Gennip, S. et al. (2025). [GLONET: Mercator's
  end-to-end neural global ocean forecasting system](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JH000686).
  *Journal of Geophysical Research: Machine Learning and Computation*, 2(3).
- Lellouche, J.-M. et al. (2021). [The Copernicus Global 1/12° Oceanic and Sea Ice GLORYS12
  Reanalysis](https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2021.698876). *Frontiers in Earth Science*, 9.
  *(The reference reanalysis.)*

## Forecast verification

- Ryan, A. G. et al. (2015). [GODAE OceanView Class 4 forecast verification framework: global
  ocean inter-comparison](https://doi.org/10.1080/1755876X.2015.1022330). *Journal of
  Operational Oceanography*, 8(sup1).
- Rasp, S. et al. (2024). [WeatherBench 2: A benchmark for the next generation of data-driven
  global weather models](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023MS004019). *Journal of Advances in
  Modeling Earth Systems*, 16(6).
- Gneiting, T. and Raftery, A. E. (2007). [Strictly proper scoring rules, prediction, and
  estimation](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437). *Journal of the American
  Statistical Association*, 102(477). *(The CRPS.)*
- Ferro, C. A. T. (2014). [Fair scores for ensemble forecasts](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.2270).
  *Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society*, 140(683). *(The fair CRPS.)*
- Fortin, V. et al. (2014). [Why should ensemble spread match the RMSE of the ensemble
  mean?](https://doi.org/10.1175/JHM-D-14-0008.1) *Journal of Hydrometeorology*, 15(4).
  *(The spread-skill ratio and its finite-ensemble factor.)*

- Pujol, M.-I. et al. (2016). [DUACS DT2014: the new multi-mission altimeter data set

- Verron, J. et al. (2015). [The SARAL/AltiKa altimetry satellite
  mission](https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2014.1000471). *Marine Geodesy*, 38(sup1).
- Lambin, J. et al. (2010). [The OSTM/Jason-2 mission](https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2010.491030).
  *Marine Geodesy*, 33(sup1). *(Jason-3 is its successor.)*
- Durand, M. et al. (2010). [The Surface Water and Ocean Topography mission: observing
  terrestrial surface water and oceanic submesoscale eddies](https://doi.org/10.1109/JPROC.2010.2043031).

- Morrow, R. et al. (2019). [Global observations of fine-scale ocean surface topography with the

- Fu, L.-L. et al. (2024). [The Surface Water and Ocean Topography mission: a breakthrough in

- Roemmich, D. et al. (2009). [The Argo Program: observing the global oceans with profiling

- Wong, A. P. S. et al. (2020). [Argo data 1999–2019: two million temperature-salinity profiles
